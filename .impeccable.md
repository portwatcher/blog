## Design Context

### Users

Regular internet users, with or without a technical background, who visit the blog to read articles and browse its archive without needing specialized knowledge.

### Brand Personality

Minimalist, spacious (留白), and content-centric. The interface should feel quiet and direct, keeping attention on the writing.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing light, editorial presentation with generous negative space and restrained controls. New interface elements should remain visually secondary to article titles and content.

### Design Principles

- Keep content as the strongest element on every page.
- Use generous whitespace to establish hierarchy and pace.
- Prefer familiar, self-explanatory interactions for a broad audience.
- Add visual detail only when it improves orientation or usability.
- Preserve the established typography, palette, and understated character.
